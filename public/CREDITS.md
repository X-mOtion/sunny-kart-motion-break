# Sunny Kart · Asset and dependency credits

This list covers resources in the game package. File paths and checksums are recorded in assets/manifest.json and pose/source-manifest.json.

## Kenney · CC0 1.0

Author: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl). Original license texts are preserved in licenses/.

| Asset pack | Used in this game | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Toy Car Kit | vehicle-racer kart, packaged as assets/kart.glb | https://kenney.nl/assets/toy-car-kit |
| Blocky Characters | character-a driver and drive animation | https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters |
| City Kit (Suburban) | Six house models | https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban |
| Nature Kit | Three tree models | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit |
| Particle Pack | smoke_03 backup texture; active smoke is generated procedurally | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack |

Modifications: textures embedded in GLB; model sizes and kart/house colors adapted at runtime; driver body, arms, helmet, numbers and exhausts customized. Roads, barriers, street lights, signs, start arch, distant scenery, clouds, interface, smoke, sparks, exhaust flames, glints, confetti, speed rays, boost and overtake sounds are generated by game code.

## Racing engine loops · CC0 1.0

Author: domasx2. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops

loop_3_0.wav converted to 22.05 kHz mono PCM WAV as assets/audio/engine.wav. Playback rate changes with driving speed.

## Pure Raceway · CC0 1.0

Author: MintoDog. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pure-raceway

Normal race and climax tracks converted to 96 kbps MP3 as assets/audio/race.mp3 and final.mp3.

## Car tire squeal skid loop · CC BY 3.0

Original recording: **audible-edge (Tom Haigh)**. Loop editing: **qubodup**.

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Modifications: tires_squal_loop.wav converted to 22.05 kHz mono PCM WAV as assets/audio/drift.wav. Volume follows the amount of wheel slip. This attribution, source, license link and modification notice remain with the game package.
