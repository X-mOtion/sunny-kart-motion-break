# Sunny Kart — Install & Play

**[Install from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sunny-kart-motion-break/fmghnfigogeolhhmpbnpceiiaichepoa)** · [中文图文教程](docs/INSTALL.zh-CN.md)

Three quick steps on a desktop computer.

### 1. Install in Chrome

Open the store link above in **Google Chrome**. Click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension** in Chrome’s confirmation.

### 2. Pin Sunny Kart

Click Chrome’s **puzzle icon**, then the **pin** next to **Sunny Kart - Motion Break**.

![Pin Sunny Kart in the extensions menu](docs/images/install/05-pin.png)

### 3. Open & play

Click the **Sunny Kart** icon in the toolbar. The game opens in a new tab with **Motion** selected. Click **Let us cruise** and allow camera access.

![Sunny Kart opened and ready to play](docs/images/install/06-ready.png)

Keep your head and both shoulders in view. Tilt your head to change lanes, lean your shoulders to turn, and shrug to boost. You can also choose **Keyboard** to play without a camera.

Chrome keeps the store-installed extension up to date automatically. The screenshots show Chrome on a Mac; button text follows your browser’s language.

Prefer the ZIP version? Follow the [manual installation guide](docs/INSTALL.manual.en.md).
